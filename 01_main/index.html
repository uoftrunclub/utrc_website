Who we are

Built for
everyone
who runs.

UTRC was founded in January 2018 as a social, drop-in, student-led organization dedicated to fostering an inclusive and supportive running environment — guided by our slogan, “No one left behind.”

Our mission is to ensure that all members, regardless of pace or experience level at the University of Toronto, unite to enjoy the act of running. We do that by providing organized, accessible and safe running programs and events — from a first 5K to a Saturday café run across the city.

We draw runners from every faculty and every year. Some are chasing a marathon PB. Most just want a reason to get outside on a Wednesday and meet people who feel the same.
