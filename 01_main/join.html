How a run works

Four steps.
That’s it.

01

Show up early

Get to the meeting point about five minutes before. Look for the UTRC shirts and the very obvious crowd of a hundred-odd people. Say hi — someone will find you.

02

Run your pace

Run at whatever pace feels comfortable, not the one that sounds impressive. There is a sweeper at the back, so going steady never means going alone.

03

Follow the leader

We’ll usually split everyone into groups at the start. Feel free to join whichever one you want! There is a leader at the front and a sweeper at the back to support you in case you fall behind.

04

Stay for the food

On Saturdays, we regroup at the finish and usually end up somewhere with coffee or food. Feel free to stick around and get to know some of the other runners!

Dressing for it

What to wear,
by temperature

Dress for about ten degrees warmer than the thermometer says. You will be cold for the first kilometre and glad of it for the other four.

Above 10°C

Mild

Most of September, and again from May.

  • Short-sleeve shirt or singlet
  • Shorts
  • Cap, sunscreen and sunglasses for sunny runs
4°C to 10°C

Cool

Late autumn and early spring. The easiest running weather there is.

  • Long-sleeve shirt
  • Cropped or full-length pants
  • A light sweatshirt over the top, if you want one
  • Reflective/bright clothing for the 7 PM runs once it gets dark early
−1°C to 4°C

Chilly

Late November, and again in March. Hands and ears feel it first.

  • Long-sleeve shirt
  • Pants or tights
  • Sweatshirt or fleece over the top
  • Light gloves and ear warmers
−6°C to −1°C

Cold

A normal Toronto winter. Layers matter more than thickness.

  • Long-sleeve shirt, tights underneath if you want them
  • Fleece and pants over that
  • Lightweight jacket on top
  • Hat and heavier gloves
Below −6°C

Deep cold

Windchill does the damage, not the number. Cover everything.

  • Long-sleeve shirt and tights
  • Fleece and pants over that
  • Lightweight jacket on top
  • Hat, neck gaiter, and two pairs of gloves
