How drops work

Three
steps

01

We announce

Every drop is announced on Instagram first, with the design, the price and the window for ordering. Turn story notifications on if you don’t want to miss it.

02

You order

Orders run through a form for a fixed window — usually about a week. We only print what’s ordered, which keeps the price down and the waste at zero.

03

Pick up at a run

Kit gets handed out at a Wednesday run a few weeks later. No shipping, no delivery fee — just come and get it, and wear it on Saturday.
